Fluffy Berry Pancakes

Soft, golden pancakes stacked high with fresh berries and a drizzle of maple syrup. The perfect weekend breakfast.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Serves 3

Ingredients & Instructions

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 1/2 cups milk
  • 2 large eggs
  • 3 tablespoons melted butter
  • 1 cup fresh berries
  • Maple syrup, to serve

Instructions

  1. Mix the flour, sugar and baking powder in a large bowl.
  2. Whisk the milk, eggs and melted butter in a separate bowl.
  3. Pour the wet mixture into the dry mixture and stir until just combined.
  4. Heat a lightly oiled pan over medium heat.
  5. Pour a ladle of batter and cook until bubbles appear, then flip.
  6. Stack the pancakes, add berries and finish with maple syrup.
